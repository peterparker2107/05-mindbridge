# MindBridge — Your Campus Mental Wellness Companion 🌱
> **Student Wellness & CBT Cognitive Restructuring Platform** • **100% Client-Side In-Browser Execution**

MindBridge is an autonomous, 100% client-side mental wellness and Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) journal designed specifically for engineering students navigating the intense pressures of campus life: Day-1 placement season rejections, ₹30 Lakh fee burden anxieties, 75% attendance cutoff stress, and hostel isolation.

---

## 🌟 Core Features

### 1. 3-Step Interactive CBT Thought Record
- **Step 1: "What happened?"** — Captures the objective trigger, emotional intensity (10–100% distress slider), and automatic negative thoughts (NAT). Pre-filled with realistic TIET scenarios (Placement OA rejections, MST exam shock, 75% attendance shortage).
- **Step 2: "Catch Distortions"** — Interactive card grid unmasking 9 classic cognitive errors with tailored campus examples:
  - *Catastrophizing* ("I missed 1 OA question, my ₹30L degree is wasted")
  - *All-or-Nothing Thinking* ("If my CGPA dips below 8.5, my future is destroyed")
  - *Fortune Telling*, *Overgeneralization*, *Mind Reading*, *Emotional Reasoning*, *Mental Filter*, *“Should” Statements*, and *Personalization*.
  - Antidote inquiry questions tailored to break each specific distortion.
- **Step 3: "Rational Reframe"** — Guided cognitive restructuring to synthesize a balanced, compassionate, and realistic perspective, with a 24-hour micro-action step. Re-rates emotional distress and triggers a celebration confetti burst upon breakthrough reframing.

### 2. Longitudinal Mood Journey Timeline
- Dynamic **Recharts** emotional well-being trajectory graph tracking feelings across the semester (`Overwhelmed`, `Anxious`, `Neutral`, `Relieved`, `Confident`).
- Interactive custom tooltips showing mood emoji, campus pressure tags, and personal reflection notes.
- Instant mood check-in logger and campus stressor analytics (identifies top pressure points: Placements, MSTs, Fees, Attendance, Hostel).

### 3. The Grounding Oasis (Somatic Breathwork & Panic Defusal)
- **4-7-8 Parasympathetic Reset** — Expanding and contracting SVG animation with real-time breathing phase cues (Inhale 4s → Hold 7s → Exhale 8s) and cycle counter.
- **Web Audio Chimes** — Synthesized calm harmonic frequencies (528 Hz) rendered 100% client-side without any audio file downloads.
- **4-4-4-4 Box Breathing** toggle for interview focus.
- **5-4-3-2-1 Sensory Grounding Technique** — Interactive somatic checklist to rapidly halt acute panic attacks by anchoring to campus sensory surroundings.

### 4. Campus Crisis Safety Shield
- Permanent top alert strip and accessible emergency modal with direct one-click dial and copy-to-clipboard:
  - **TIET Campus Health Center & Counseling:** `0175-2393000` / Ext `2345`
  - **iCall Psychosocial Helpline (TISS):** `9152987821`
  - **Vandrevala Foundation 24x7:** `9999 666 555`
  - **Tele-MANAS (Govt of India):** `14416`
  - **AASRA 24x7:** `+91 98204 66726`
- Transparent legal and clinical disclaimer: MindBridge is a peer self-reflection tool, not medical treatment.

### 5. Campus Resources & Sanctuaries Hub
- Complete guide to the TIET Dispensary, Dean of Student Affairs (DoSA) medical certificate policies, and quiet campus study sanctuaries (Central Library 3rd floor, COS Lawns, Nirvana garden).
